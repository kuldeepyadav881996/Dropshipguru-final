About DropshipGuru

Helping students, professionals, homemakers and business owners across India launch and grow profitable online businesses through complete, done-for-you ecommerce solutions.

Who We Are

DropshipGuru is owned and operated by DropshipGuru Private Limited. We are an India-based ecommerce enablement company that helps beginners and growing sellers start, set up, and scale their online businesses across leading marketplaces and their own branded stores.

From marketplace seller-account setup and product research to store development, branding, marketing support, and one-on-one mentorship, our mission is to make selling online simple, transparent, and achievable for everyone — regardless of their background or prior experience.

What We Do

DropshipGuru Private Limited provides end-to-end digital ecommerce and business-support services, including:

  • Seller account setup on Amazon, Flipkart, and Meesho;
  • Shopify store setup and custom ecommerce website development;
  • Product research, listing creation, and catalogue management;
  • Branding, creative, and digital marketing support (Meta & Google Ads);
  • Business consultation, mentorship, and structured mastery courses;
  • Ongoing account management and dispatch support.

Our Mission

We believe entrepreneurship should be accessible to everyone. Our goal is to remove the technical and operational barriers that stop people from starting an online business, and to support our clients with genuine guidance, fair pricing, and reliable execution at every step of their journey.
