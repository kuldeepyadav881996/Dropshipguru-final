    Frequently Asked Questions
    Is this program suitable for beginners? ▾
    Yes! Our program is designed for complete beginners. We start from scratch and guide you step by step through product research, account setup, and your first sale.
    How soon can I set up my store? ▾
    Most students set up their store within 7–15 days of joining. With our step-by-step guidance and support, you'll be ready to start selling — though results depend on your own effort and market conditions.
    What is included in the One Year Support? ▾
    You get a dedicated account manager, product listing support, order processing help, monthly strategy calls, and 24/7 WhatsApp support for the full year.
    Do I need any investment to start? ▾
    Dropshipping requires minimal investment. You only pay after you receive an order from the customer. We teach you exactly how to start with zero inventory.
    Is the Webinar live or recorded? ▾
    The webinar is 100% live with real-time Q&A. You interact directly with our experts, ask questions, and get personalized answers during the session.
    Are there any hidden charges? ▾
    Absolutely not. What you see is what you pay. There are zero hidden charges, no recurring fees, and no surprises. Full transparency, always.
